Why not a subscription?

A database client is a tool you keep for years and open every day. Paying for it every month makes sense for the seller and for nobody else. Taula charges once because that is what a good tool costs, and because a paid-up customer is a customer we do not have to keep re-selling to.

There is no free tier and no trial build to expire on you. The download is the real app; the license is what makes it yours. If it turns out not to be for you, the refund policy below is short.

How the license works

Four steps, none of them a form.

  1. Pay once

    Card or the usual wallets, through the checkout page. You get a receipt you can expense.

  2. Get the email

    The download link and your license key arrive within a minute. Keep the email; it is the whole license.

  3. Install

    Open the DMG, drag Taula to Applications, paste the key once. That one call activates the Mac — no account, no password, nothing else sent.

  4. Use it everywhere you work

    The same key on your laptop and your desktop, three at a time. Retiring a Mac? Release it from your account page and the seat is free again.

Requirements

What it needs.

macOS 15 or laterTaula is a native Swift app and uses APIs that arrived with macOS 15.
For the local assistantllama.cpp from Homebrew and a few gigabytes for one model. Apple silicon with 16 GB is comfortable; everything else in Taula works without it.
For backupsThe Postgres client tools from Homebrew for pg_dump. SQLite needs nothing extra.

Buying questions

Is it really one payment?

Yes. €25 once buys Taula on three of your Macs at a time, with every future update included. There is no subscription and no renewal fee.

Do I need an account?

No account, no password. Your licence key arrives by email, unlocks your download page, and activates the app itself — the key is the whole account.

Can I try it first?

There is no separate trial build. If you buy it and it is not for you, write within 14 days and you get a refund, no questions beyond "what was missing".

Does my team need one license each?

A license is per person, not per Mac. Five people, five licenses. Write to us for more than ten and we will sort out an invoice.

Can I get an invoice with VAT?

Yes. Enter your company details at checkout and the receipt is a valid invoice. VAT is applied according to your country.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 15 or later. The optional local assistant needs llama.cpp and runs best on an Apple silicon Mac with 16 GB of memory.

Something wrong? Reply to your receipt.

Polar's receipt email comes from the shop that sold you adar, and a reply reaches the person who wrote it. Include the last five characters of your key and what you were doing.

Before you write

I lost my licence key

It is in Polar's receipt email and in your Polar customer portal, which you can open with the email you paid with. Signing in to your downloads needs nothing else.

The app says my licence is in use on three Macs

Sign in to your downloads and release one of the Macs listed there. The seat frees immediately, and the Mac you release asks for the key again the next time it opens.

The app says it cannot check my licence

It keeps working for 14 days without reaching us. Connect the Mac to the internet once and the warning clears on its own; nothing about your repositories is sent.

I want a refund

Reply to the receipt within 14 days. The licence is revoked when the refund goes through, and the app locks on its next launch.

The inbox stays empty

Run gh auth status, then adar probe from a terminal. The probe fetches every inbox bucket, one pull request and your notifications with timings, changes nothing, and its output is the most useful thing you can paste into an email.

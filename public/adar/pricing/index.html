Why not a subscription?

A review tool is something you open fifty times a day and keep for years. Paying for it every month makes sense for the seller and for nobody else. adar charges once because that is what a good tool costs.

There is no free tier and no trial build to expire on you. The download is the real app; the licence is what makes it yours. If it turns out not to be for you, the refund policy below is short.

How the licence works

Four steps, none of them a form.

  1. Pay once

    Card or the usual wallets, through Polar's checkout. You get a receipt you can expense.

  2. Get the email

    Your licence key arrives within a minute. Keep the email; it is the whole licence.

  3. Install

    Sign in to your downloads with the key, open the DMG, drag adar to Applications and paste the key once. That one call activates the Mac. Nothing about your repositories is ever sent.

  4. Use it everywhere you work

    The same key on your laptop and your desktop, three at a time. Retiring a Mac? Release it from your account page and the seat is free again.

Buying questions

Is it really one payment?

Yes. €9 once buys adar on three of your Macs at a time, with every future update included. There is no subscription and no renewal fee.

Do I need an account?

No account, no password. Your licence key arrives by email, unlocks your download page, and activates the app itself. The key is the whole account.

What does activation send?

Your key, and five fields about the Mac: a hashed hardware fingerprint, its name, its model, the macOS version and the adar version. Never a repository, a branch, a pull request or a line of code.

Can I try it first?

There is no separate trial build. If you buy it and it is not for you, write within 14 days and you get a refund.

Does my team need one licence each?

A licence is per person, not per Mac. Five people, five licences. Write to us for more than ten and we will sort out an invoice.

Can I get an invoice with VAT?

Yes. Enter your company details at checkout and the receipt is a valid invoice. VAT is applied according to your country.
